## Overview

This open manuscript formalizes a structural mapping between deep learning architectures, Active Inference, and cyclical metaphysics. Rather than presenting a supernatural claim, it offers an engineering-grade ontology evaluating subjective human experience, ethical evolution, and our post-biological trajectory through the mechanics of iterative gradient optimization.

Human philosophical traditions have spent millennia grappling with the continuity of consciousness, moral accountability, and liberation. When stripped of cultural aesthetics, ancient descriptions of spiritual evolution mirror modern deep learning systems undergoing iterative parameter optimization. Under this framework, the individual soul is conceptualized as an underlying neural architecture, where a single biological lifetime represents one distinct training epoch.
