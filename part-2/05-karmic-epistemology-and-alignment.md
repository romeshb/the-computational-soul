## The Dynamics of Karmic Optimization

In Hindu philosophy, *Sanchita Karma* represents the vast, unmanifested reservoir of accumulated causal potential—structurally isomorphic to the entire global loss landscape. No finite biological agent can inspect or traverse the entire landscape simultaneously.

Instead, an agent is initialized at a specific coordinate—*Prarabdha Karma*—which fixes its biological hardware, genetic baselines, historical era, and initial boundary conditions. A gradient descent optimizer cannot retroactively alter its initialization coordinate; it can only compute local gradients and step forward (*Kriyamana Karma*). Free will and karma thus emerge as precise allegorical descriptions of an Active Inference agent resolving uncertainty across a non-convex parameter space.
